Write Khmer · Android

Support

How do I practice?

Choose a character category, open a lesson, follow the stroke guide, and trace on the screen. Lessons work offline.

How do I reset my stars?

Open Settings in the app and choose Reset Stars. This removes saved lesson scores from your device.

Why are notifications or ads missing?

Notifications need permission and a configured push service. Ads may be unavailable in some regions or while the app is in test mode.

Contact support

Email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com with the app name, your Android version, and a short description of the problem. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive data.
