Write Khmer · Android

Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026 · Package: com.agrokh.iwritekhmer

This policy explains how the Write Khmer Android app, published by GarudaOne, handles data. You can use the lessons without creating an account.

Data stored on your device

Lesson stars, sound and music settings are stored on your device. The app uses them to remember your progress and preferences. You can reset stars in Settings, clear the app’s data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Data sent to our Parse service

When a network connection is available, the app may register a Parse installation and send an app-open event. After a lesson, it sends the character, lesson category, and earned stars. Parse may also receive technical information such as an installation identifier and IP address needed to process requests. We use this information to operate notifications and understand lesson use.

If push messaging is configured and you grant notification permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Parse may process a device push token to deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off in Android Settings.

Advertising

When enabled, Appodeal and its advertising partners display banner and interstitial ads. They may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad impressions and clicks, and performance information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. The app is configured for child-directed treatment and mature-content filtering. Appodeal’s privacy policy explains its processing and choices.

Sharing and retention

We share the data described above with Parse, Firebase when push is configured, and Appodeal and its ad partners when ads are enabled. We do not require a user account. Device data remains until you reset or clear it. Parse installation and event records remain on our server until service cleanup or a deletion request; no fixed automatic expiry is currently set. Advertising partners retain data under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

You can decline notification permission, reset lesson stars, clear app data, and use Android’s advertising privacy controls. For privacy questions or a request to delete records we control, email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com. Include the app name and enough information to locate your request; do not send sensitive information in a public post.
