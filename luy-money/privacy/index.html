LUY Money Manager · Android

Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026 · Package: com.khmermobile.expense

This policy explains how the LUY Money Manager Android app, published by Khmer Entertainment Apps / GarudaOne, handles data.

Data stored on your device

Your personal expense and income transactions, categories, accounts, and currency preferences are stored locally on your device. You can edit or delete transactions in the app, clear the app’s data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app at any time.

Optional cloud backup and account sync

In versions that offer optional cloud backup, you may sign in with your email address or Telegram account to back up and restore your expense records across your devices via our Parse server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Your backup data is private to your account.

Advertising and in-app purchases

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) displays banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, diagnostic information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details. In-app purchases (such as ad removal) are processed securely by Google Play Billing.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal or financial transaction records. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the app (Google AdMob for advertising, Google Play Billing for purchases, and our cloud backup service if you choose to sign in). Local device data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Data deletion and contact

You can clear local app data or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings. To request deletion of any cloud account or backup records we control, or for any privacy questions, email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com with your account email address.
