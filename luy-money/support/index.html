LUY Money Manager · Android

Support

How do I track expenses and income?

Add transactions by selecting a category, account, and amount in USD ($) or Khmer Riel (៛). Filter your spending summary by week, month, or year.

How do I delete my data or account?

Local records can be deleted directly inside the app or by clearing app storage in Android Settings. If you use cloud backup and want your cloud account and backups permanently deleted, email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com from your registered email address.

Contact support

Email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com with the app name, your Android version, and a short description of your question.
