Khmer Wallpaper Plus · Android

Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026 · Package: com.khmerapp.kwallpaper

This policy explains how the Khmer Wallpaper Plus Android app (com.khmerapp.kwallpaper), published by Khmer Entertainment Apps / GarudaOne, collects, uses, and shares user data.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite wallpapers and display preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear the app’s data in Android Settings or uninstall the app at any time.

Advertising and analytics

When ads are enabled, third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads) display banner and interstitial ads. These SDKs may collect and share device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements and analyze app performance. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Data security, sharing, and retention

Data transmitted by the app and its SDKs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with advertising and analytics service providers needed to operate the app. Local device data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Your choices, data deletion, and contact

You can clear local app data or reset/delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings at any time. If you have privacy questions or wish to request deletion of any data we control, email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com.
