Child safety standards & CSAE policy

Last updated November 20, 2026 · Package: khmerfun.khmerfriends

Met Laor - Khmer Friends is intended solely for users aged 18 and older. We have a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Published safety standards

Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, or soliciting any content that exploits, abuses, or endangers children. Any violation results in immediate removal of content, permanent account/access termination, and reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities.

Reporting and compliance contact

Users can report any inappropriate content or suspected child safety violation directly to our designated Child Safety Point of Contact at khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com. We review and act on all child safety reports expeditiously in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.
