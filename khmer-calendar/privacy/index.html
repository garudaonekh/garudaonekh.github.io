Khmer Calendar Pro · Android

Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026 · Package: com.agrokh.khmercalendar

This policy explains how the Khmer Calendar Pro Android app, published by GarudaOne, handles data. You can use the calendar without creating an account.

Calendar and reminders

Calendar and holiday data are included with the app. Your custom alarm titles, dates, times, and notification settings are stored on your device. The app uses them to schedule local notifications; it does not upload those reminder details to our server. Android may deliver the scheduled notifications. You can disable notifications in the app or Android Settings.

Advertising

When enabled, Appodeal and its advertising partners display banner and interstitial ads. They may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad impressions and clicks, and performance information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Appodeal’s privacy policy explains its processing and choices.

Sharing and retention

We do not operate a calendar account or upload custom reminders. Appodeal and its ad partners process the advertising data described above when ads are enabled. Local reminders and settings remain until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Advertising partners retain data under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

You can disable reminders and notifications, delete custom alarms, clear app data, and use Android’s advertising privacy controls. For privacy questions or deletion requests about data we control, email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com. Do not send sensitive information in a public post.
