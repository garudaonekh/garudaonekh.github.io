Khmer Calendar Pro · Android

Support

Which dates are available?

The current Android app includes calendar data from 2022 through 2027. Choose a month or tap Today to navigate.

How do I manage reminders?

Tap a date to add a custom alarm. Use the notification settings button to change holy-day reminders and their time. Allow Android notification permission when prompted.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check notification permission, your reminder settings, and Android battery restrictions for the app. Some devices limit background alarms.

Contact support

Email khmermobiledeveloper@yahoo.com with the app name, your Android version, and a short description of the problem. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive data.
